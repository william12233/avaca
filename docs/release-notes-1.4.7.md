# AVACA 1.4.7

AVACA 1.4.7 adds personal actress ratings and sortable home-gallery rankings.

## Highlights

- Rate each actress from 1 to 10 for face, chest, figure, and on-screen appeal.
- Sort the home gallery by each category or the average, with rank and score
  badges shown on the cards.
- View and edit aliases from the actress details while keeping them available
  for work search and scraping.
- Preserve ratings in data exports and imports; older archives remain
  compatible.
