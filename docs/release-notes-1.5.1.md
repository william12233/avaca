# AVACA 1.5.1

## Highlights

- Save, import, and delete actions now show a compact, rounded message at the
  bottom of the screen. Its colors follow your theme.
- Messages wrap on narrow screens, dismiss after three seconds, and show the
  latest result when actions happen in quick succession.
- Operation results use shorter wording. Scrape-job errors now appear in your
  selected language.
