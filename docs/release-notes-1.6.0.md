# AVACA 1.6.0

## Highlights

- Open **Settings → Appearance** to preview and apply the Default or Frosted
  Glass theme. AVACA remembers your choice on restart.
- Both themes support your custom palette and the AMOLED black setting.
- Use **Motion Effects** to control interface transitions. The preview shows
  how panels expand and collapse with the setting on or off. AVACA also
  respects your system's reduced-motion preference.
