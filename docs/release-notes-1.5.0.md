# AVACA 1.5.0

## Highlights

- Tap **AVACA** on the home screen to draw a random work from your entire local
  collection. The dialog shows the work's thumbnail, code, and title.
- The rating section in the home filter and sort sheet now opens and closes
  without the horizontal divider lines.
