# AVACA 1.4.2

AVACA 1.4.2 refines the Settings help affordances so option labels and their
trailing controls are easier to scan.

## Highlights

- Moved all static help indicators to the right side of their labels.
- Kept a consistent 8px gap before switches and expansion controls.
- Preserved tap and mouse-hover tooltip behavior with regression coverage.
